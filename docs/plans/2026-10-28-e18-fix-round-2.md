Source: the run's signoff visit result (`status: recording_failed`), its stop reason "event 1 fails the event schema at /scenario: ... should not be valid under {'pattern': ' · '}", and the run window's report. The owner: "Yes to fix 1 and fix Tim d 2" (yes to the ruling on the live run's slice; fix round 2), 2026-10-28.

The two defects:

1. A quotation holding the records separator breaks recording late. 2. A refused recording cannot be redone. The session restated the two scenarios and ran `record-answer` again, but `record` replayed the batch it had already receipted and was refused again. The run could not recover, though nothing had landed.

## Slice A - a quoted separator never breaks recording

Status: not started

Requirements:

- When signoff-v2 writes a finding or kept note to the records log, the verdict doc or the punch list, every ` · ` inside its claim, scenario or other single-line text field is written as ` - `. The verdict doc says once, in plain words, that a quoted separator was written as a hyphen. `answer.json` in the run directory keeps the original.
- `record-answer` checks every event the run would append against the records event schema before it accepts the answer, so an answer that would be refused at `record` is refused at `record-answer` instead, naming the field and the rule, with nothing receipted.
- A new test, `scripts/tests/test_fr2_separator.py`, records a finding whose claim and scenario quote a `/fb` line: the run records and exits 0 (fails on today's code), the records log and verdict doc hold the hyphen form, and `answer.json` holds the original. A second test feeds an answer that breaks another records rule and shows `record-answer` refuses it with nothing receipted.
- No other change.
