# Smoke doc — the corner bar's happy-hour sign

A one-page scope note used only to exercise readers. Nothing in it is real.

Intent: put a small chalkboard sign by the front door of the bar that tells walk-ins
what happy hour is today, so the door staff stop answering the same question.

Decisions:
- The sign is a chalkboard, hand-written each afternoon by whoever opens — decided.
- It lists the drink specials and the hours — decided.
- It is put out at opening and brought in at close — decided.
- The sign faces the sidewalk — decided.

Open:
- "Happy hour runs until the game starts" — the doc never says which game, or what
  happens on a night with no game. A cold reader should be able to name this gap.

Acceptance:
- On a weekday at 5 pm the sign is outside and readable from the far curb.
- The door staff report fewer than three "what's happy hour?" questions per shift.

Out of scope:
- A printed or lit sign — the owner wants chalk.
- Posting the specials online — a separate idea.
