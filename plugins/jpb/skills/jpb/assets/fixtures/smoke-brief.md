# Brief (fixture — smoke tests only)

Working name: BrineButler. BrineButler is a small countertop device for home cooks who ferment
vegetables. It holds one wide-mouth jar, keeps the contents at a chosen
temperature, vents gas automatically, and shows days-elapsed and a simple
ready estimate on a small display. It needs no app; three physical controls
cover everything. It is aimed at people who have ruined a batch or two and
want repeatability without becoming hobbyist chemists.
